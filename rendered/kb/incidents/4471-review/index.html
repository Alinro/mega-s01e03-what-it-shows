Parser: the shared parser reads month first

In the exported incident, day-first dates arriving through app, email, or booking-v2 were interpreted month first. For example, R-41200 reports 7 April becoming 4 July. The desk now reports the dd/mm parsing fix. Suggested verification covers app, email and booking-v2 paths, including ambiguous dates and invalid month/day combinations; this review did not run product tests.

Hint: booking-v3 prints the wrong format

In the exported incident, booking-v3’s hint said MM/DD/YYYY, but the form read dates in the tenant’s day-first locale. A person following the hint could submit a date that the form interpreted differently. R-41241 is an alder.physio form report, even though its browser locale is en-US. The desk chose not to fix the hint because booking-v3 was retired. Any replacement form should keep its hint, validation and parsing consistent with the tenant’s locale.

Fixes and verification belong in separate GitHub issues in this repository; their bodies are in issues/parser.md and issues/hint.md. The analysis file lists every report ID allocated to each issue.
