Triage: duplicate reports

owner support desk current in this kb revision reviewed 2026-11-09 · 0 days ago

Keep one ticket per evidenced problem. Decision 0002 replaces subject-only matching. Decision 0001 is preserved as history.

The rule

  1. Read the body and resolve the tenant in the tenant directory. Check its locale and form version, and the report’s channel.
  2. Compare cause and affected path with existing tickets. Same subject alone is insufficient; different subjects can still be duplicates.
  3. For #4471, keep the shared-parser and booking-v3 hint failures apart. Use the review and classifier to check allocation.
  4. Investigate unknown tenants, contradictory descriptions and one-day/timing anomalies. Do not merge them automatically because a subject matches.
  5. Preserve the report ID, received time and move history. Deduplicate export rows by ID, then count. Refresh the export before claiming a current queue total.
The explorer is a classification aid, not a substitute for reading the evidence. A component's classification does not prove a fix or resolve contradictory report bodies.
